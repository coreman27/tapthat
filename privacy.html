DON'T TAP THAT

Privacy Policy · Last updated 29 August 2026

Short version: DON'T TAP THAT collects nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no trackers. Everything the game remembers stays on your device.

Information we collect

We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. The app has no user accounts, no login, and no backend that receives your data.

Information stored on your device

To make the game work, the app stores the following locally on your device only (using local storage). This data never leaves your phone and is not accessible to us or anyone else:

  • Your best score.
  • An optional nickname you choose to label your score.
  • Game settings and preferences.

Deleting the app removes this data.

Analytics & tracking

None. We do not use analytics SDKs, advertising identifiers, cookies, or any form of cross-app or cross-site tracking.

Sharing

The "Challenge a friend" feature uses your device's native share sheet. If you choose to share, the message is sent by you, through the app you pick (Messages, Mail, etc.). We never see or store what you share or who you share it with.

Children's privacy

The app is safe for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
