DON'T TAP THAT

Privacy Policy · Last updated 1 October 2026

Short version: Game scores, your nickname, and adaptive gameplay statistics stay on your device unless you choose to share a score or, where available, join the optional weekly leaderboard. The free iOS app uses Google AdMob for ads. Apple handles the optional one-time Remove Ads purchase. The browser game does not include the native advertising or purchase SDKs.

Game data

The game has no user accounts. Unless you choose to join the optional weekly leaderboard (described below, only in versions where it is available), nothing about your play is sent to a developer-operated server. We do not send your nickname, score, or adaptive gameplay statistics to an advertising service. Advertising and purchases involve the separate third-party processing described below.

Information stored on your device

To make the game work, the app stores the following locally on your device only (using local storage):

  • Your best score.
  • An optional nickname you choose to label your score.
  • Game settings and preferences.
  • Challenge-category success and failure statistics for adaptive difficulty.
  • Ad timing settings, a locally assigned timing variant, session/run counts, and recent ad-attempt times used to enforce frequency limits.

Deleting the app removes its local game data. Your purchase remains associated with your Apple Account and can be restored.

Advertising in the free iOS app

The free iOS version may show an occasional full-screen Google AdMob ad between games. The app decides when to offer a break using completed runs and new personal bests, with session and time limits. This decision stays on the device; these scores are not sent to Google to personalize ad content. The app requests non-personalized ads and does not request Apple's tracking permission. Non-personalized advertising does not mean that no data is collected.

Google's advertising SDK may process IP addresses (including approximate location inferred from them), device or app identifiers, ad views and interactions, and crash and performance diagnostics to deliver ads, measure performance, and prevent fraud. We do not request precise device location. For Google's practices, see the Google Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads data disclosures.

Google's consent flow is used where required before requesting ads. When available, open Remove ads & purchases > Ad Privacy Choices to review your choices. The game remains playable when consent or ad loading is unavailable. Buying Remove Ads stops future ad requests after the entitlement is verified; it does not erase information already processed by Google. There is no separate gameplay analytics SDK.

Optional local ad-timing diagnostics

Under Remove ads & purchases > Local ad timing diagnostics, you can choose to record ad opportunities, outcomes, and whether and how quickly you start another run. This is off by default. Records include timestamps, local run/session numbers, timing variant, score-milestone categories, and ad-free status, not your nickname, raw scores, or payment details. They are kept on this device for up to 30 days, with a maximum of 300 finished-run records, and are pruned when the diagnostics are next used.

No diagnostic report is automatically uploaded. You can view and manually copy a report, clear the measurements, or turn recording off to delete them. Frequency-limit state remains so clearing measurements does not cause extra ads. If you choose to send a report to someone, that recipient can read the timing information it contains.

Purchases

Remove Ads is an optional, non-consumable, one-time Apple in-app purchase, not a subscription. Apple processes payment and manages the purchase history under its privacy policy. The app checks Apple's verified transaction information to enable ad-free access and supports Restore Purchases. We do not receive your card details. A refund or revoked purchase can remove ad-free access.

Optional weekly leaderboard and friend groups

In versions where it is available, you can choose to join a weekly leaderboard and form private friend groups. This is off until you tap Join; nothing is created or sent before that, and the game works fully without it.

If you join, the app sends to our server (hosted on Amazon Web Services):

  • the display name you choose (letters and numbers only, up to 14 characters, filtered for inappropriate words; please do not use your real name or other personal information);
  • your weekly score and the reaction time of each round, used to check that a score is plausible;
  • a random anonymous ID and a secret key generated on your device. They are not linked to your name, email, Apple Account, or any advertising identifier;
  • friend group codes you create or join.

Other players can see your display name and score on the leaderboard and in any friend group you join. We do not collect an email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, or payment details for this feature. Our hosting provider may process IP addresses in standard infrastructure logs.

Weekly scores are deleted automatically after about ten weeks, and idle friend groups after about a year. You can delete everything now: Weekly leaderboard > Delete my leaderboard data removes your name, scores, group memberships, and anonymous ID from our server and from your device. If you are offline when you do this, the request is retried when you try again. Uninstalling the app does not by itself delete server data; use the delete option first.

Scores are checked for plausibility but are not guaranteed to be authentic. The leaderboard is a casual feature, and we may remove entries.

Sharing

If you choose to share a challenge, the message and link contain your nickname and score. They are shared through the app you choose, or copied to your clipboard. Recipients can read that information and may forward the link. We do not receive your recipient list.

Children's privacy

The app does not include an age-verification system or in-app chat. We request Google's most restrictive ad-content rating, but advertising is provided by a third party. This is not a promise that the advertising SDK collects no information about children. Parents can contact us with privacy questions and use Apple's purchase restrictions.

The optional leaderboard has no chat, free-text messages, or profile pages, and shows only a filtered display name and score. Because the app has no age verification, parents and guardians should decide whether a child should join it, and should make sure the display name is not a real name. Parents can ask us to delete a child's leaderboard data, and anyone can delete their own data in the app as described above.
