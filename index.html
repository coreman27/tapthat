How to play

  • Read the command at the top.
  • Do exactly what it says before the timer runs out.
  • Sometimes the command is DON'T do something — so don't.
  • One clean tap. Missing the button, double-tapping, or holding a tap ends the run.
  • When it says HOLD, hold until the bar fills.
  • One mistake ends the run. Then you try again. Forever.

An AI learns what you're bad at — and serves you the exact command you keep failing.
